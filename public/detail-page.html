About

Our Evening Desert Safari features an unforgettable itinerary of activities, from breathtaking sunset stops and wildlife spotting to a sumptuous BBQ buffet and stargazing in a Bedouin-inspired camp.

Duration: 6 hours Language: English
Full description

Experience the magic of the Arabian desert on this comprehensive evening safari. From the thrill of dune bashing in a 4x4 Land Cruiser to the serene beauty of a desert sunset, this tour offers a perfect blend of adventure and relaxation.

Know before you go

Please wear comfortable clothing and closed shoes. Pregnant women and people with back problems should not participate in dune bashing.

Age policy

Infants (0-2 years) go free. Children (3-11 years) require a child ticket.

Cancellation policy

Free cancellation up to 24 hours before the experience starts.
